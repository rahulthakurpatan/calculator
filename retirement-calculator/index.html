How the retirement estimate works

The calculator uses a transparent two-stage model: accumulation before retirement and withdrawals after retirement.

Before retirementFuture value = current savings growth + monthly contributions + employer contributions

Monthly compounding is used for the accumulation estimate. Fees are deducted from the assumed annual return.

After retirementClosing balance = opening balance + investment growth − portfolio withdrawals

Retirement spending increases with the inflation assumption. Pension and other income reduce the amount that needs to be withdrawn from the portfolio.

Retirement calculator FAQs

Understand the assumptions before using the results to make a financial decision.

How much money do I need to retire?

There is no single retirement number for everyone. Your target depends on spending, retirement age, planning horizon, inflation, investment returns, fees and predictable income such as pensions. Use the result as a scenario estimate, not a universal rule.

How much should I save each month for retirement?

The amount depends on your current savings, years until retirement, expected return after fees and the income you want. This calculator estimates the monthly amount needed under a simplified monthly compounding model.

Can I use this as a 401(k), pension, RRSP, superannuation, EPF or NPS calculator?

Yes, you can include the balances and contributions from those accounts in the current savings and additional account fields. The calculator does not determine account-specific tax treatment, contribution limits, access rules or government benefit eligibility.

Does it calculate Social Security, State Pension, CPP, OAS or Age Pension?

No. Enter an estimate from the relevant official statement or government service. Benefit amounts and eligibility depend on personal records and country-specific rules that this general calculator does not verify.

Does this include tax, healthcare and long-term care costs?

Enter the spending amount you want to plan for, including any healthcare, insurance, housing and care costs you expect. The tool does not calculate personal income tax or healthcare eligibility.

Are the investment returns guaranteed?

No. The return assumptions are user-selected estimates. Actual markets can rise or fall, inflation can change and withdrawals during market downturns can affect portfolio longevity. This calculator does not provide a Monte Carlo probability of success.
