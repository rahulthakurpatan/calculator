How this calculator works

Pre-ad contribution = price − COGS − shipping − processing/marketplace fees − other variable costs. Break-even ROAS = price / positive pre-ad contribution. Post-ad contribution subtracts acquisition cost. Monthly profit = post-ad contribution × delivered orders − fixed overhead.

Worked example

At a selling price of 60 with product cost 20, shipping 6, 4% processing fees (2.40) and other costs 2, pre-ad contribution is 29.60. Break-even ROAS is 60 ÷ 29.60 = 2.03×. At an ad cost of 12, contribution after advertising is 17.60 per order.

Assumptions and limitations

Inputs describe delivered orders only. Model refunds, failed deliveries, tax remittance, discounts and inventory losses in your effective cost inputs if material. ROAS uses revenue attributed to acquired orders, which may differ from platform-reported ROAS.

Calculations are educational estimates, not individualized financial advice. Verify current provider terms before making a decision.

Frequently asked questions

Why is ROAS not profit?

ROAS divides attributed revenue by ad spend and ignores product costs, fulfillment, fees and overhead.

What if contribution is negative?

No positive advertising spend can produce break-even at the stated price and variable costs.
