What these numbers mean

  • Income: all recorded sales and other business receipts.
  • Expenses: all recorded business costs and payments.
  • Net result: income minus expenses.
  • Profit margin: net profit divided by income, multiplied by 100.

How to use this Business Profit & Loss Tracker

  1. Choose whether the transaction is income or an expense.
  2. Enter its date, amount and category. Add a short description if useful.
  3. Press Add entry. Your record will appear in the table and the totals will update.
  4. Use the report period filter to review one month or all saved entries.
  5. Export the visible report to CSV for a spreadsheet backup or further analysis.

Formula used

Net profit or loss = Total business income − Total business expenses

Profit margin (%) = (Net profit ÷ Total income) × 100

If income is zero, the tracker displays the profit margin as not available rather than dividing by zero. A negative net result indicates that recorded expenses exceed recorded income for the selected period.

Example

Suppose a small business records ₹80,000 in sales and ₹55,000 in expenses during a month. Its estimated net profit is ₹25,000. The profit margin is ₹25,000 ÷ ₹80,000 × 100 = 31.25%.

Important limitations

This is a simple cash-entry tracker, not a full accounting system. It does not automatically handle inventory valuation, depreciation, unpaid invoices, loans, GST, income tax, owner drawings, or accounting adjustments. Record transactions consistently and consult an accountant for formal books, tax returns and statutory reporting.

Frequently asked questions

How is business net profit calculated?

Net profit in this tracker is total recorded income minus total recorded expenses for the selected period. The result depends on the completeness and accuracy of your entries.

Where are my business records saved?

Entries are saved in this browser on this device using local storage. They are not synced to an online account. Clearing browser data, using private browsing, or changing devices may make the records unavailable. Export a CSV backup regularly.

Can I export my profit and loss records?

Yes. Select a report period and use Export CSV. The download includes the entries shown for that selected period and can be opened in spreadsheet software.

Does this tracker replace accounting or tax software?

No. It is a basic record-keeping and estimation tool. It does not prepare statutory financial statements, calculate GST or income tax, or replace advice from a qualified accountant.
