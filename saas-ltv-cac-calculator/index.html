How to interpret your results

Benchmarks are directional context, not universal pass-or-fail rules.

LTV:CAC around 3:1

A 3:1 gross-profit LTV to CAC ratio is a widely used SaaS rule of thumb. A good target depends on growth strategy, retention, capital and customer mix.

Shorter CAC payback

A shorter payback period generally returns acquisition cash sooner. Self-serve businesses often aim for faster payback than enterprise contracts with longer sales cycles.

Watch churn assumptions

Small changes in churn can materially change simplified LTV. Compare this estimate with actual customer cohorts and retention trends before making decisions.

SaaS LTV:CAC calculator FAQs

Understand the assumptions behind customer lifetime value and acquisition payback.

How do you calculate SaaS customer lifetime value?

A common simplified formula is monthly average revenue per account multiplied by gross margin, divided by monthly customer churn. It assumes revenue, margin and churn remain stable. Cohort-based LTV can be more accurate when retention and expansion change over time.

Should LTV use revenue or gross profit?

For SaaS unit economics, gross-profit LTV is generally more useful because it accounts for the direct cost of delivering the service. Revenue LTV is shown as a secondary metric for context.

What is a good LTV to CAC ratio?

A 3:1 gross-profit LTV to CAC ratio is a commonly used rule of thumb, not a universal target or guarantee. Consider payback, growth, retention, funding and customer segment as well.

How is CAC payback calculated?

Divide customer acquisition cost by monthly gross profit per customer. This simplified estimate assumes monthly gross profit is stable and excludes financing costs, taxes and changes in customer behavior.

Does this calculator use customer churn or revenue churn?

The core LTV estimate uses customer (logo) churn. Revenue churn and net revenue retention can behave differently, particularly when existing customers expand or contract their subscriptions.

What costs should be included in CAC?

Depending on your reporting policy, include relevant sales and marketing payroll, commissions, advertising, agencies, campaign costs and acquisition-related tools. Use a consistent definition and match the spend period to the new customers acquired.

Can I use this calculator for annual subscriptions?

Yes. Select annual ARPA billing and enter the annual recurring revenue per account. The calculator converts it to a monthly equivalent for the simplified monthly LTV and payback formulas.

Are these results a forecast?

No. The simple model assumes stable ARPA, gross margin and customer churn. It does not model customer cohorts, expansion revenue, contraction, discounting, taxes, refunds or changes in pricing. Use actual cohort data for detailed forecasting.
