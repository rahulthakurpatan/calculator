How the country profiles work

Select the country where you are tax resident and use the profile that matches the relevant tax year. These are simplified educational calculations.

United States

Digital assets are generally treated as property. This version uses FIFO as a default approximation, separates short-term and long-term holding periods, and applies the rate you enter to the simplified taxable gain. Specific identification is not implemented.

United Kingdom

Uses same-day matching, acquisitions in the following 30 days, then a simplified Section 104 pool for remaining units. It does not calculate the annual exempt amount or income-band interactions.

Canada

Uses average-cost Adjusted Cost Base (ACB) per asset and a 50% capital-gains inclusion assumption for an individual capital-property scenario. Business income treatment is not modelled.

Australia

Uses FIFO parcels and flags individual parcels held for more than 12 months for a potential 50% CGT discount. Eligibility and other tax adjustments must be checked separately.

India

Uses a simplified VDA transfer model: positive gain per disposal is included without offsetting losses from other disposals. It estimates 30% tax plus 4% cess; surcharge and other individual circumstances are excluded.

Not included yet

Exchange imports, wallet matching, crypto-to-crypto swaps, mining, staking, airdrops, DeFi, NFTs, derivatives, gifts, business-income classification and loss carryovers.

Crypto capital gains formula

For a disposal, the basic calculation is sale proceeds after eligible selling costs minus the allocated cost basis.

Capital gain or loss = Net disposal proceeds − Allocated cost basis

Acquisition cost basis generally starts with quantity × purchase price plus eligible acquisition costs. The exact treatment of fees and other costs depends on the tax jurisdiction.

FIFO means first-in, first-out. It is not the correct universal method: the UK uses matching and pooling rules, while Canada generally uses average-cost ACB for capital property.

Frequently asked questions

Is FIFO or LIFO allowed in every country?

No. Tax rules vary. The UK has same-day, 30-day and Section 104 matching rules; Canada generally uses average-cost ACB for capital property. This calculator does not present LIFO as a universal option.

Are crypto-to-crypto swaps included?

Not in this first version. A crypto-to-crypto exchange can be a taxable disposal in several jurisdictions and needs its own transaction valuation and cost-basis handling.

Does this calculate my final tax bill?

No. It estimates gains and, where you provide a marginal rate or use the simplified India profile, an indicative amount. Tax bands, allowances, surcharges, losses, business treatment and local rules can change the result.

Are transfers between my own wallets taxable?

A genuine transfer between wallets you own is generally not a disposal by itself in the covered profiles. Record the transfer for reconciliation, preserve the original acquisition date and cost basis, and do not enter it as a new purchase.

Can I upload an exchange CSV?

Not yet. This version uses manual entries and provides a downloadable calculation report. Exchange CSV imports and wallet transfer matching are planned as later improvements.
