What Is Crypto Staking & APY Compounding?

Crypto staking generally means committing or delegating tokens to help a proof-of-stake blockchain operate. Depending on the network and staking method, participants may receive additional tokens as rewards. The reward rate is not a guaranteed bank interest rate: it can change with network economics, validator performance, protocol rules, commissions and other conditions.

DeFi yield is a broader term. It can include lending, liquidity provision and other smart-contract strategies. These activities are not identical to native network staking and can carry different risks. In particular, USDT and USDC are stablecoins; yield on them usually comes from a DeFi or service-provider strategy rather than native proof-of-stake validation.

APR vs APY: why the difference matters

APR is a nominal annual rate before compounding. APY expresses the effective annual yield after compounding. If a 5% APR is restaked monthly, the effective annual yield is slightly above 5%. If a platform already quotes 5% APY, that figure already includes the annual compounding effect. Applying another APY conversion on top of it would overstate the return.

Effective APY = (1 + APR ÷ n)n − 1
Here, n is the number of reward-restaking periods in one year.

How Staking Rewards Are Calculated

This calculator first converts your starting investment into token units using the starting token price. It then models rewards using your selected rate type, restaking frequency and duration. The calculation assumes the entered rate remains constant for the full period and every reward is successfully restaked at the selected interval.

Compound token balance = Initial tokens × (1 + periodic rate)number of periods

For an APR quote, periodic rate equals APR divided by the number of compounding periods per year. For an APY quote, the periodic rate is derived from the effective APY so that the annual yield is not counted twice. The simple-interest comparison uses the equivalent nominal APR, which makes the two growth paths comparable.

Token price growth is a separate assumption

Staking can increase the number of tokens you hold while the market price of each token falls. To illustrate that difference, the optional price scenario compounds your assumed annual token-price change separately from staking rewards. The projected portfolio value is the final token balance multiplied by the projected token price. A positive staking balance does not guarantee a positive return in your chosen currency.

Projected portfolio value = Final token balance × Projected token price

Pro Tips to Consider Staking Yields More Safely

  • Understand the source of yield. Check whether the rate comes from native protocol rewards, lending interest, liquidity incentives or a temporary token reward.
  • Check validator and provider terms. Review commission, uptime, custody arrangements, lock-up periods, withdrawal queues and unbonding delays.
  • Review smart-contract and counterparty risk. DeFi deposits can be exposed to code vulnerabilities, admin controls, exploits and service-provider failure.
  • Do not compare APY alone. Consider token volatility, inflation, fees, slashing, liquidity and whether the displayed rate is variable or promotional.
  • Keep records for tax reporting. Tax treatment of rewards differs by country and can depend on when rewards are received, controlled or disposed of.
  • Never share wallet secrets. A calculator should not ask for a seed phrase, private key or wallet password. This tool does not connect to wallets.
Important: This calculator is a mathematical scenario tool, not a validator estimator or a DeFi protocol risk model. It does not predict network rewards or verify whether a staking service is safe.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the difference between staking APR and APY?

APR is a nominal annual rate before compounding. APY is the effective annual yield after compounding. The calculator lets you select which type of rate you are entering so it does not compound an APY a second time.

2. Does daily compounding always produce higher rewards?

For the same nominal APR, more frequent restaking generally produces a slightly higher effective APY. If the rate is already quoted as APY, the APY already includes compounding and changing the modeled interval does not increase the annual effective rate.

3. Are staking rewards guaranteed?

No. Actual rewards can change with protocol parameters, validator performance, network activity, commissions and provider terms. Some networks also have slashing risk, lock-up periods or unbonding delays.

4. Can I calculate USDT or USDC yield?

Yes. Select USDT or USDC and enter the yield rate offered by the strategy you are evaluating. These are stablecoins, so the yield generally comes from lending or another DeFi/service arrangement, not native proof-of-stake staking. Depeg, smart-contract and counterparty risks may apply.

5. Does this tool use live prices, live APY or calculate tax?

No. Enter your own starting token price and reward-rate assumption. The calculator does not fetch live market data, apply foreign-exchange conversion, include protocol fees or estimate taxes. Actual tax rules vary by country.

Know what the estimate excludes

The model is intentionally transparent. It does not include:

  • Variable future staking rates or validator commission changes
  • Gas, transaction, withdrawal or platform fees
  • Slashing, failed validators or lost rewards
  • Lock-up and unbonding delays
  • Smart-contract, custody, lending or depeg losses
  • Taxes, inflation of token supply or fiat exchange-rate movement

Use the output as a scenario, not a promise or recommendation to stake.
