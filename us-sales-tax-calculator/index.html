US sales tax calculator for receipts, checkout and invoices

Sales tax is commonly added to a taxable purchase at checkout in the United States. A shopper, small business, reseller or bookkeeper may need to calculate the tax to charge on a pre-tax price—or extract the tax portion from a receipt that already includes it. This free tool handles both operations, plus optional quantity, pre-tax discount, shipping, and two rounding conventions. It works in US dollars and accepts state and local rates separately.

Sales tax formulas

Combined rate = (State rate + Local/district rate) / 100 Forward sales tax = Taxable base × Combined rate Tax-inclusive total = Pre-tax total + Sales tax Reverse pre-tax price = Tax-inclusive price / (1 + Combined rate) Reverse included tax = Tax-inclusive price − Reverse pre-tax price

When reversing a receipt, do not simply subtract 8.25% of the gross amount. The percentage applies to the pre-tax base, not the tax-inclusive price. A discount is applied after recovering the pre-tax unit price, so a nonzero discount changes the modeled transaction rather than reproducing the original receipt exactly.

Worked example: reverse an 8.25% sales tax

A $108.25 tax-inclusive item at an 8.25% combined rate contains a $100.00 pre-tax price and $8.25 of sales tax. To recover the pre-tax price, divide $108.25 by 1.0825. If you instead calculate 8.25% of $108.25, you would overstate the tax portion. For a forward example, a $125.00 taxable item at 8.25% has $10.31 of tax after illustrative cent rounding and a $135.31 final total.

How do state and local sales tax rates work?

The state tax rate is only one part of the rate in many US jurisdictions. County, city, transit or special district rates may apply. The Tax Foundation's 2026 Facts & Figures state and local tax table provides statewide rates and population-weighted average local rates as of January 1, 2026. A population-weighted average is not an address-specific checkout rate, and rates may change after the report's effective date.

For example, California's statewide base rate in the cited January 2026 table is 7.25%, but district taxes can raise the applicable combined rate. Five states do not impose a general statewide sales tax—Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon—although certain local or specialized taxes can still apply. This calculator does not maintain a jurisdictional tax-rate database. Obtain the actual rate from the relevant state department of revenue or a current receipt.

When should shipping be taxable?

Shipping and delivery charges are treated differently across states and circumstances. Select taxable shipping only when the shipping charge is part of the taxable base for the specific transaction. The tool adds shipping to the pre-tax total in either case, but applies sales tax to it only when you select “Yes.” It does not model multiple taxability categories within the same invoice.

Unit rounding versus line rounding

Tax calculations can differ by cents when each unit is rounded independently rather than calculating tax on the entire extended line. For instance, if an item generates a fractional-cent tax and is sold in quantity, rounding each unit first can differ from rounding once on the aggregate. Select the convention appropriate for your POS or invoicing system. The calculator uses illustrative half-up-style cent rounding for nonnegative tax amounts, not a claim of legal compliance with every state's tax-rounding rules.

Tax-sensitive purchase planning

The rate-sensitivity chart shows seven hypothetical combined tax rates. It is useful for understanding how a tax change could affect the total purchase cost while holding item price, discount, quantity and shipping fixed. It does not predict future tax rates or establish tax liability.

Limitations and important cautions

  • Taxability: Groceries, medicines, services, digital goods, clothing and other categories can have special exemptions or rates.
  • Sourcing: The destination, origin, buyer and seller nexus may affect which tax is owed.
  • Discount treatment: Manufacturer coupons and seller discounts can have different tax consequences; this tool assumes a straightforward pre-tax seller discount.
  • Shipping: Verify whether shipping is included in the taxable base.
  • Split display: The state and local tax components shown are a proportional allocation of the final rounded total. Actual invoice allocation may differ by cents.
  • Not filing software: This tool does not file returns, check resale certificates, or decide registration and remittance obligations.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove sales tax from a total?

Choose “Reverse sales tax,” enter the tax-inclusive per-unit price and the applicable combined state plus local rate. The tool divides by one plus the rate, then shows the estimated net and tax amounts.

What if my state has no statewide sales tax?

Enter 0% for state tax and the verified local rate, if any. Some jurisdictions impose local taxes despite having no general statewide sales tax.

Is the local tax rate always a fixed percentage?

No. Different city, county, district, item and delivery rules can apply. The model assumes a single entered ad valorem rate on the entire taxable base.

Does this calculate VAT or GST?

It uses percentage-tax arithmetic, but it is specifically designed for US sales tax receipts. VAT/GST invoice credit, tax-inclusive pricing rules and filing requirements are outside its scope. Use our GST calculator for a different tax context.
