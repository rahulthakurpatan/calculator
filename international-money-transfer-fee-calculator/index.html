How this calculator works

Each provider's sender-side fixed and percentage fees are deducted from the amount before conversion. The entered provider rate is applied, then any recipient-currency fee is subtracted. Effective cost converts the final payout back using the entered reference rate. A negative markup is possible when a provider offers a better-than-reference rate.

Worked example

Sending 1,000 at a reference rate of 1.12 gives a no-fee benchmark of 1,120 recipient units. Provider A with a rate of 1.10, an 8-unit fixed fee and 1% percentage fee delivers (1,000 − 8 − 10) × 1.10 = 1,080.20 units.

Assumptions and limitations

This is a comparison of user-entered quotes, not live exchange-rate or remittance-provider data. Fees may depend on funding method, amount, transfer route, intermediary banks and time. Compare actual final quotes.

Calculations are educational estimates, not individualized financial advice. Verify current provider terms before making a decision.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool show live FX rates?

Why does the best provider change?

Fixed fees, percentage fees, the exchange rate and recipient deductions all affect the final payout.
