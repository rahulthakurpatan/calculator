    How the Income-tax Act transition affects TDS

    The Income-tax Act, 2025 came into force on 1 April 2026. For non-salary payments, the applicable Act is generally determined by the earlier of the credit or payment event. Transactions triggered on or before 31 March 2026 continue under the Income-tax Act, 1961; events from 1 April 2026 use the new Act. Salary TDS is deducted at payment, so the salary payment date determines the applicable framework.

    Most TDS rates and monetary thresholds were retained, but the new Act reorganised non-salary provisions into Section 393 tables. For tax-year 2026–27, quoting an old section such as 194C instead of the applicable Section 393 table item may create reporting mismatches.

    Form 141

    Form 141 consolidates specified challan-cum-statements previously filed through Forms 26QB, 26QC, 26QD and 26QE. It covers only listed transactions and eligible deductor categories; it is not a universal TDS return. Schedule E was introduced from 1 October 2026 for specified non-resident property-transfer cases.

    Important calculation limitations

    • Annual aggregate, payer classification, recipient status and payment-specific conditions can change applicability.
    • For NRI payments, DTAA relief, rates in force, surcharge, cess and the precise income classification need separate analysis. This page does not automatically calculate NRI statutory rates.
    • Missing PAN may trigger higher-rate provisions. A valid lower/nil deduction certificate may override the normal rate.
    • Thresholds may apply to aggregate sums or monthly payments. This tool cannot reconstruct your full ledger or prior-period deductions.
    • Special-rate income, exemptions, reimbursements, GST treatment, joint payees and complex transactions require professional review.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which Act applies to TDS after 1 April 2026?

    The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies to relevant credit/payment events on or after 1 April 2026. For non-salary payments, the earlier of credit or payment generally determines the trigger.

    Have TDS rates changed under the new Act?

    The Income Tax Department states that rates and monetary thresholds have been retained; the main change is the simplified and consolidated presentation of TDS provisions.

    What is the new section for TDS?

    Salary TDS is covered by Section 392. Most other TDS provisions are consolidated under Section 393, which contains separate tables for different payee categories.

    Does Form 141 apply to every TDS payment?

    No. It is for specified transactions and schedules, including eligible rent, property, contractor/professional and VDA transactions. Other deductions are reported through the applicable TDS statement process.

    Can I calculate TDS for an NRI?

    This tool flags non-resident cases and permits an adviser-supplied indicative rate, but it does not independently determine Section 393(2), treaty eligibility or rates in force.

    Is this an official TDS calculator?

    No. It is an independent educational estimator. Always verify the applicable law, rules, notifications, PAN status and certificate before deducting or depositing TDS.
