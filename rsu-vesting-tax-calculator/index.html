How RSU vesting taxes work

For US federal tax purposes, the fair market value of shares delivered at vesting is generally treated as wage income. Your employer may sell or withhold shares to cover estimated withholding, but withholding is not necessarily your final tax bill. Your actual liability depends on annual income, deductions, filing status, payroll wages and state or local rules.

Sell-to-cover estimate

This calculator estimates incremental tax from the equity compensation using the marginal rates you enter, adds an approximate employee payroll tax component, then estimates a whole-share sale quantity. It is a planning estimate, not an employer payroll instruction.

ISO and Alternative Minimum Tax

Incentive stock option exercise can create an AMT adjustment when the exercise spread is included in alternative minimum taxable income and shares are held beyond the exercise year. The AMT calculation on a real return includes many items beyond ISO spread, including deductions, exemptions, phaseouts and credits.

ISO exercise cash

Exercising options requires paying the strike price. A large paper gain does not mean the shares can be sold at the same price later. Consider liquidity, concentration and expiration risk before exercising.

How NSO taxes work

For nonqualified stock options, the difference between exercise-date fair market value and strike price is generally compensation income at exercise. A later sale may create an additional capital gain or loss based on the adjusted basis and sale price. Actual payroll and withholding treatment can depend on the plan and employment circumstances.

Federal, state and payroll assumptions

Federal marginal income tax, state/local marginal tax and payroll tax are estimated separately. This tool does not calculate a full federal return, itemized deductions, credits, special state rules, nonresident allocations or every payroll exception. State rates are editable assumptions, not authoritative tax tables.

Frequently asked questions

Does RSU withholding equal my final tax?

No. Withholding is a prepayment. Your final tax depends on your full-year tax return, so you may owe more or receive a refund.

How many shares should I sell to cover RSU taxes?

The tool divides its estimated incremental tax by the vest-date share value and rounds up to a whole share. Your broker or employer may use a different withholding method.

Does every ISO exercise trigger AMT?

No. The exercise spread can increase alternative minimum taxable income, but whether AMT is due depends on the complete tax calculation.

Does this calculator include all 50 state tax systems?

No. State and local tax are represented by an editable marginal-rate assumption. Special state rules, including state AMT and multi-state sourcing, are outside this version.

Does it calculate capital gains tax on a future sale?

It shows the basic post-exercise price difference for options when a sale price is entered, but it does not determine holding-period qualification or a complete capital-gains tax liability.
