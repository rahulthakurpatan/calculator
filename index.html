How to calculate your ROI

Use these three simple steps to understand the return generated by an investment.

💰

1. Enter your investment

Add the total amount you originally invested. Use the same currency for both amounts.

📈

2. Enter the final value

Enter what the investment is worth now, or the amount you received when you sold it.

🗓️

3. Add the time period

Choose the number of years or months. The calculator estimates the annualized return too.

ROI formula

Return on investment compares the gain or loss with the original amount invested.

Return on InvestmentROI (%) = ((Final Value − Amount Invested) ÷ Amount Invested) × 100

Annualized return (CAGR) = ((Final Value ÷ Amount Invested) ^ (1 ÷ Years)) − 1
