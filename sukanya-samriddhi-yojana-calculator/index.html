How the Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana calculator works

SSY is a Government-backed small-savings scheme for a girl child's future. Its deposit window and maturity period are different.

15-year deposit window

Deposits can be made for 15 years from account opening. You do not need to deposit for all 21 years.

21-year maturity

The account normally matures 21 years after opening. The remaining six years can earn interest without fresh deposits.

Quarterly notified rate

The Government reviews small-savings rates quarterly. Constant-rate projections are illustrations only.

SSY interest calculation: why deposit date matters

Interest for a month is calculated on the lowest balance between the close of the fifth day and the end of that month. Interest is credited on 31 March each financial year. Depositing before the fifth day can therefore make a difference compared with depositing later.

Monthly interest ≈ eligible lowest monthly balance × annual rate ÷ 12

This tool models monthly eligible balances, accumulates interest during the financial year and credits rounded interest at year-end. It is a planning model, not the bank or post office ledger. Actual posting dates, notified rates, rounding and account records prevail.

Minimum and maximum deposit

The minimum is ₹250 per financial year and the maximum is ₹1,50,000 per financial year. Deposits are made in multiples of ₹50. Monthly instalments are not compulsory; the yearly limit applies across all deposits in that financial year.

Annual step-up

Test a 5%, 10% or custom annual contribution increase. The calculator checks the yearly total against the ₹1.5 lakh ceiling. An increase is optional and should fit your budget.

Education withdrawal from Sukanya Samriddhi

Eligible education withdrawal may be made after the girl reaches 18 years or passes Class 10, whichever happens earlier. The ceiling is 50% of the balance at the end of the preceding financial year, and the withdrawal cannot exceed documented actual education expenses. Admission or fee documents are required.

The scheme permits a lump sum or instalments subject to prescribed conditions, no more than one withdrawal in a year, for up to five years. The calculator's one-year estimate is indicative only. The account office verifies age, education and expense documents. Marriage closure and compassionate premature closure have separate conditions.

SSY tax benefits under the Income-tax Act, 2025

Eligible contributions may qualify for deduction under Section 123 read with Schedule XV within the combined ₹1,50,000 ceiling. This is not an extra SSY-only deduction. The deduction is not available under the new tax regime under Section 202; any benefit depends on your tax regime and remaining deduction capacity.

Qualifying payments from an account opened under the Sukanya Samriddhi Account Scheme, 2019 are excluded from total income under Schedule II. Tax treatment depends on the law for the relevant tax year and your circumstances. This is general information, not tax advice.

Eligibility and opening an SSY account

  • The account must be opened before the girl completes 10 years of age.
  • Generally, one account may be opened for each of up to two girl children in a family. Special provisions apply in qualifying twin or triplet birth situations.
  • A parent or legal guardian opens the account at an authorised post office or participating bank.
  • Keep date-of-birth proof, guardian identity/address documents and required forms ready. The institution may request additional KYC.
  • After the girl turns 18, she operates the account under the prescribed documentation process.

Confirm family-account exceptions, residency requirements and KYC with the authorised institution. Official scheme rules prevail.

SSY versus PPF and fixed deposits

SSY is designed for a girl child and has a long lock-in linked to account opening. PPF is a broader long-term savings scheme with different tenure, contribution limits and withdrawal rules. Bank FDs have bank-specific rates and tenures but do not share SSY's scheme conditions. Compare liquidity, tax treatment and time horizon—not only the headline rate.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current SSY rate in November 2026?

The reference rate used here is 8.2% for October–December 2026. Rates are notified quarterly and can change.

Is 8.2% guaranteed for 21 years?

No. It is the current quarter's rate. The calculator holds it constant only for illustration.

Can I deposit ₹1.5 lakh every year?

Yes, subject to eligibility. The limit is per financial year, not per month, and deposits must be in multiples of ₹50.

Do I have to deposit every month?

No. Monthly instalments are not compulsory. You must meet the minimum annual deposit requirement.

What happens if I miss the minimum deposit?

The account may be treated as default. It can generally be regularised within the prescribed period by paying ₹50 for each default year plus the minimum deposit due for each such year. Confirm arrears with the account office.

Can I withdraw half the balance for college?

Eligible withdrawal is limited to 50% of the preceding financial year's closing balance and actual documented education expenses. Age and documentary conditions apply.

When does SSY mature?

Normal maturity is 21 years from account opening. Deposits are accepted only for the first 15 years.

Can I calculate an existing account?

Yes. Enter the current passbook balance and its as-of date. The tool projects future activity and does not recreate historical interest.

Is SSY deduction available in the new tax regime?

No. Section 123 deduction is not available under the new tax regime. Under the eligible regime, it shares the combined ₹1.5 lakh ceiling.
