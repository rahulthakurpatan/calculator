How Amazon FBA fees work

Fulfilment by Amazon (FBA) lets sellers send inventory to Amazon fulfilment centres, where Amazon can store, pick, pack and ship orders. FBA unit economics have more than one fee: a referral fee on eligible sales, a fulfilment charge depending on product attributes, and storage charges based on inventory volume and time. Depending on your marketplace, additional fees may include inbound placement, closing charges, returns, aged inventory or low-inventory surcharges. Amazon's official Revenue Calculator can help sellers obtain a product-specific fee estimate.

Many product categories have different referral rates, minimums or tiered calculations. In this tool the referral fee is modeled as the higher of your entered percentage of net sales and your entered minimum fee. It is an intentionally simplified manual-fee scenario, not an official Amazon rate lookup. Verify the current fee in your marketplace's Seller Central before changing pricing.

How to calculate Amazon FBA net profit, margin and ROI

Landed cost = product manufacturing + freight to Amazon + prep and packaging + import duties. Amazon fees = referral + fulfilment + optional surcharge + storage + closing + inbound placement + other fees. Ads = net sales revenue × entered ACoS. Monthly overhead per unit = recurring overhead ÷ monthly units.

Net profit per unit = net product revenue − landed cost − Amazon fees − PPC cost − expected return loss − allocated overhead. Net profit margin = net profit ÷ net product revenue × 100. Inventory ROI = net profit ÷ landed cost × 100. Monthly net profit = unit net profit × projected monthly units.

Inventory ROI and margin measure different things: inventory ROI uses only landed inventory capital as the denominator, while all seller expenses are already subtracted from the profit numerator. If landed cost is zero, ROI is undefined and the calculator displays N/A rather than an infinite figure.

Break-even and target-margin pricing

Break-even price is the lowest modeled selling price at which estimated unit profit reaches zero. A target-margin price makes unit profit at least the chosen percentage of net revenue. The calculator numerically solves both thresholds, accounting for the selected referral fee minimum and tax-inclusive selling prices. It returns “Not feasible” when percentage-based charges leave too little revenue for the requested margin.

Worked example — private-label product

Consider a $30 item with $8 manufacturing cost, $1.20 inbound shipping, 15% referral fee, $4.20 fulfilment, $0.35 storage, 10% ACoS, a $0.75 returns allowance, $0.30 other Amazon fees, and $40/month overhead on 200 units. Under those manually entered assumptions, the total unit cost is $22.50, profit is $7.50, margin is 25%, inventory ROI is about 81.52%, and projected monthly profit is $1,500. These values do not represent an official Amazon fee quote.

Pro tips to reduce FBA fees and improve profitability

  • Confirm size and weight tiers: packaging size changes can affect the FBA fulfilment fee. Check current Seller Central dimensions.
  • Compare seasons: Amazon may adjust fulfilment and storage rates by selling season. Do not assume annual rates remain fixed.
  • Keep inbound and storage realistic: account for inventory held before sales, placement charges and aged inventory fees.
  • Measure advertising efficiency: ACoS directly affects contribution margin. Include advertising even when a product appears profitable before PPC.
  • Track returns and taxes: estimate nonrecoverable return costs once. VAT/GST and recoverable input taxes require seller-specific accounting.

In 2026, Amazon's US FBA fulfilment guidance identifies a fuel/logistics surcharge and holiday-peak fee period. The optional surcharge field should be set to zero if your entered fulfilment fee already includes that charge. Amazon India has a separate fee schedule, and EU/UK rules differ again. Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate Amazon FBA profit per unit?

Start with the selling price excluding any included sales tax or VAT, then subtract your landed product cost, referral and fulfillment fees, storage, advertising, expected returns and allocated monthly overhead.

Does Amazon FBA charge both referral and fulfillment fees?

Usually yes. Referral fees relate to selling through Amazon, while FBA fulfillment fees cover picking, packing and shipping through Amazon's logistics network. Actual rates vary by marketplace, category, size and weight.

What is a good Amazon FBA profit margin?

There is no universal good margin. Compare net profit after every relevant cost, working capital requirements, return risk, competition, advertising spend and your desired investment return.

How is Amazon FBA ROI different from profit margin?

Profit margin divides net profit by net sales revenue. Inventory ROI divides net profit by the landed product acquisition cost, so the percentages answer different questions.

Are the Amazon fees in this calculator official estimates?

No. You enter the applicable referral rate and fulfillment, storage and other fees yourself. Check current Seller Central or Amazon's official Revenue Calculator for your product and marketplace.
