Bond Yield to Maturity & Price Calculator

Find the yield that discounts every future fixed coupon and principal repayment to the market price. See current yield, effective annual yield and duration-based interest-rate sensitivity.

YTM formula and assumptions

Bond price = Σ coupon / (1 + y/f)k + face value / (1 + y/f)N, where y is annual nominal yield, f is coupon frequency and N is the whole number of coupon periods until maturity. A bracketed bisection solver finds y, including negative yields above −f. Current yield is annual coupon divided by market price. Effective annual yield is (1+y/f)f − 1.

Worked example

A $1,000 face-value bond paying 5% coupons twice a year for ten more years and trading for $950 has 20 remaining $25 coupons plus $1,000 at maturity. Its YTM exceeds the 5% coupon rate because the investor also gains $50 if the issuer pays par at maturity.

Day-count and settlement limitations

Settlement is assumed to occur exactly on a coupon date. Remaining coupon periods must be a whole number. This model has zero accrued interest; input price is the full amount paid at settlement and equals clean price on that date. It does not implement Actual/Actual, Actual/365, 30/360 or ex-coupon settlement conventions, stub coupons, call features, amortizing bonds, floating rates, default probability, tax or transaction costs. For bonds purchased between coupon dates, use a broker calculator that models the specific instrument and its day-count convention. The model assumes full and timely coupon/principal payments and is not investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

Why is yield to maturity different from coupon rate?

YTM accounts for both the coupon payments and the discount or premium paid relative to face value.

What does modified duration mean?

Modified duration estimates the approximate percentage price change for a one-percentage-point yield move, ignoring convexity. Exact repriced scenarios are shown above.

Does the result include accrued interest?

No. The calculation assumes settlement exactly on a coupon date; accrued interest is zero by construction.
