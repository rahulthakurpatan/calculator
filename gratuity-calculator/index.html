How gratuity is calculated in India

For an employee covered by the statutory gratuity formula, the standard calculation is:

Gratuity = Last-drawn eligible monthly wages × 15 × counted years of service ÷ 26

For the service calculation, a part of a year exceeding six months is generally rounded up to a full year. The calculator uses calendar dates and applies this rule to completed service.

What changed under the Labour Codes?

The Code on Social Security, 2020 and the revised wage definition became effective from 21 November 2025. The wage definition includes specified components and may add back the excess when excluded components exceed 50% of total remuneration. This calculator uses the basic, DA and retaining allowance fields plus the user-entered excluded-component adjustment. Some remuneration categories and statutory exclusions require employer/payroll interpretation.

Who is eligible?

  • Regular employees generally need five years of continuous service, except death or disablement cases.
  • Fixed-term employees are eligible for proportionate gratuity on expiry of the contract; Ministry guidance explains eligibility after one year of service under the fixed-term contract.
  • For death or disablement, the five-year condition does not apply.
  • Coverage, continuity, breaks in service and employer classification can affect the result.

Gratuity tax exemption

For non-government employees, the tax exemption is subject to the applicable statutory formula and overall notified ceiling (commonly ₹20 lakh under the currently applicable framework). Government employee gratuity may be fully exempt subject to the relevant service and tax provisions. This simplified result treats the actual gratuity as the amount received and does not model all prior gratuity payments or every employee-specific tax condition.

Do not rely on the disputed “4 years and 240 days” interpretation as a universal eligibility guarantee. Confirm eligibility with HR or a qualified labour-law professional.

Frequently asked questions

Is gratuity available after one year?
Fixed-term employees have a specific pro-rata entitlement under the Code and Ministry guidance. The general five-year rule continues for regular employees, subject to exceptions.

Does the calculator use 15/26?
Yes, for the standard statutory covered-employee formula. Other categories or non-covered employment may require a different calculation.

Is the full gratuity tax-free?
Not always. Tax exemption depends on employee category, applicable formula, statutory ceiling and past payments.

Can I use this for a private company?
Use it as an estimate where the establishment is covered by gratuity law. Confirm the establishment's coverage and wage structure.
