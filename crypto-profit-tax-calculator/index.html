How this crypto calculator works

Understand the difference between your investment return and the amount that may be taxable in India.

1. Crypto profit and ROI

The profit mode calculates the result of one buy-and-sell position after the trading fees you enter.

Net result = (Sell price × quantity − sell fee) − (Buy price × quantity + buy fee)

ROI is net result divided by total purchase cost including the buy fee. A positive result means a gain; a negative result means a loss.

2. Indian VDA tax estimate

The tax mode calculates positive transfer gains separately for each entered transaction. A loss-making transfer is shown separately rather than subtracted from gains.

Indicative gain per transfer = Sale consideration − cost of acquisition

It applies the 30% base VDA rate to the sum of positive gains, then adds the entered surcharge rate and 4% cess on tax plus surcharge. This is a simplified estimate.

3. 1% crypto TDS

Where applicable, VDA TDS is generally 1% of transfer consideration, not 1% of profit. Annual thresholds differ by payer category.

Estimated TDS = 1% × sale consideration, when selected threshold is crossed

The calculator estimates TDS from the total entered sale consideration. Actual TDS credit should be checked against Form 26AS / AIS and exchange or buyer records.

4. Why the two results differ

Trading fees affect the investment-performance result, but ordinary transaction expenses are not automatically deductible in the VDA tax calculation. The tax mode therefore does not subtract entered trading fees.

Likewise, VDA losses generally cannot be used to reduce gains from another VDA transfer. Keep a complete transaction ledger and seek professional advice for complex cases.

Indian crypto tax: key points for Tax Year 2026–27

A plain-language overview of the rules relevant to common crypto transfers. Check current official law before relying on an estimate.

Tax on income from transfer of a virtual digital asset

India applies a special 30% tax rate to income from transfer of a virtual digital asset (VDA), subject to the applicable statutory provisions. Health and education cess is generally 4% of income tax plus surcharge, if any. The final liability may also depend on surcharge, other income, reporting, and individual facts.

Loss set-off restriction

The VDA provisions restrict set-off of a loss from transfer of one VDA against income from another VDA transfer or other income. The tax calculator therefore does not net loss-making trades against profitable trades. It reports them separately to avoid suggesting an unsupported tax deduction.

1% TDS and annual thresholds

For Tax Year 2026–27, the Income-tax Act, 2025 places the VDA TDS rule in Section 393(1), Table Sl. No. 8(vi). Where applicable, the rate is generally 1% of consideration. The annual threshold is ₹50,000 when the payer is a specified person and ₹10,000 for other payers. A specified person generally means an individual/HUF meeting the statutory turnover/receipts or no-business-income conditions. See the Income Tax Department TDS rates for Tax Year 2026–27.

Official reading: Income Tax Department – Taxation of Virtual Digital Assets and TDS rates for Tax Year 2026–27. This page is an estimate tool, not a tax opinion. The calculator does not determine whether a payer qualifies as a specified person.

Crypto profit calculator FAQs

Quick answers about Bitcoin profit, cryptocurrency ROI, VDA tax and TDS in India.

How do I calculate Bitcoin profit?

Multiply the Bitcoin quantity by the sale price to find sale value. Subtract the purchase value and the buy/sell fees you entered. The result is your estimated trading profit or loss before tax.

How do I calculate crypto ROI?

Divide net trading profit or loss by the total purchase cost including the entered buy fee, then multiply by 100. ROI describes investment performance; it is not the same as taxable VDA income.

Is crypto profit taxed at 30% in India?

Income from transfer of a VDA is generally subject to a 30% special tax rate under the applicable VDA provisions, plus applicable surcharge and cess. Confirm the law and your facts for the relevant tax year.

Is 1% TDS deducted on crypto profit?

No. Where the provision applies, 1% TDS is generally calculated on the consideration for transfer, not on the profit. Annual thresholds and payer conditions apply.

What are the crypto TDS thresholds in India?

The commonly applicable annual thresholds are ₹50,000 for a specified person and ₹10,000 for other persons. Whether a payer qualifies as a specified person and how the threshold applies should be checked against current law.

Can I set off Bitcoin loss against Ethereum profit?

VDA loss set-off is restricted. This calculator does not deduct a loss on one VDA transfer from a positive gain on another. Review the applicable statutory provision and consult a tax professional for your facts.

Are crypto exchange fees deductible from taxable gains?

Do not assume that exchange, brokerage or other transaction fees are deductible from VDA income. The tax mode deducts only the acquisition cost in its simplified calculation; the profit mode separately includes the fees you enter for performance tracking.

Does crypto-to-crypto exchange count as a transfer?

An exchange of one VDA for another can involve a transfer and may have tax and TDS implications. The single-trade tax mode can estimate a sale consideration if you have a supportable INR value, but complex swaps need professional review.

Does this tool calculate staking, mining, airdrop or gift tax?

No. These can involve separate income, valuation and gift-tax rules. This version focuses on buy-and-transfer transactions and does not automatically classify staking rewards, mining receipts, airdrops or gifts.

Does the calculator use live Bitcoin prices?

No. Enter your own buy price, sale price and quantity. Manual inputs avoid presenting delayed or unverified exchange prices as live market data.

Can I use this calculator for USDT or other altcoins?

Yes. Select Other VDA or enter the relevant asset in your own records. The calculator uses the values you enter; it does not fetch coin prices or verify whether a token qualifies as a VDA.

Will this calculator prepare my income tax return?

No. It does not file an ITR, verify Form 26AS/AIS, import exchange statements or give personalised tax advice. Use it as a preliminary estimate and reconcile all transactions with official records.
