How to use this crypto portfolio calculator

Start with the quantity you own and a current market price for every coin in your wallet or exchange account.

1. Enter each crypto holding

Add Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana or any other token as a separate row. Enter the quantity you currently own and its current price in the selected currency. Use the same currency for every row.

2. Add your average purchase price

Enter your average buy price per coin if you want to estimate your cost basis and unrealized profit or loss. If you have bought the same coin at multiple prices, use your own average acquisition price from your transaction records.

3. Choose future growth assumptions

Select a projection period and set annual growth rates for bear, base and bull cases. You can use negative growth for a downside scenario. These rates are editable assumptions, not forecasts from the website.

4. Review portfolio allocation

The holding-wise breakdown shows each asset's current value and its percentage of your total portfolio. This helps you understand whether your holdings are concentrated in one coin or spread across several assets.

What the crypto growth scenarios mean

Scenarios help compare possible outcomes without pretending anyone knows the future price of Bitcoin or another coin.

Bear case

A lower or negative annual growth assumption. It helps you see how your portfolio value could shrink if the market performs poorly over the selected period.

Base case

Your middle or planning assumption. It is not the most likely outcome by default; it is simply the annual rate you choose for a moderate scenario.

Bull case

A stronger annual growth assumption that illustrates an optimistic outcome. Do not treat this value as a target, guarantee or expected return.

Why estimates can be very different from reality

Crypto markets can move sharply, and individual tokens can lose most or all of their value. The calculator uses a constant compounded annual rate for simplicity. It does not model volatility, drawdowns, staking rewards, taxes, fees, new purchases or withdrawals.

Crypto portfolio calculation formulas

All calculations use the values you enter. No exchange connection or hidden price assumptions are used.

Current value of one holding

Current holding value = Quantity held × Current price per coin

Current total portfolio value

Portfolio value = Sum of current values of all entered holdings

Invested amount and unrealized profit / loss

Cost basis = Quantity held × Average buy price
Unrealized P/L = Current holding value − Cost basis
Unrealized return (%) = Unrealized P/L ÷ Cost basis × 100

Future portfolio value

Future value = Current portfolio value × (1 + annual growth rate)number of years

For example, a portfolio currently worth ₹1,00,000 would become ₹1,33,100 after three years at a hypothetical 10% annual compound growth rate. This is a mathematical illustration, not a crypto market prediction.

Crypto Portfolio Calculator FAQs

Answers about current holdings, unrealized gains and future crypto value estimates.

How do I calculate the current value of my Bitcoin?

Multiply the Bitcoin quantity you own by the current Bitcoin price. For example, if you hold 0.01 BTC and enter a price of ₹60,00,000, the estimated holding value is ₹60,000.

Can I calculate my complete crypto portfolio?

Yes. Add a separate row for each cryptocurrency, including BTC, ETH, SOL, stablecoins or other tokens. The calculator adds all holding values using the selected display currency.

What is unrealized profit or loss in crypto?

Unrealized profit or loss is the difference between the current market value of an asset you still hold and its purchase cost. It is not a realized gain or loss because you have not sold the holding.

What if I bought the same cryptocurrency at different prices?

Enter your average buy price per coin based on your own transaction records. This calculator does not import exchange trades or calculate FIFO, specific-lot cost basis or tax cost basis.

Does this calculator show live crypto prices?

No. Prices must be entered manually. Check your exchange or a reliable market data provider and update the price inputs when you want a fresh portfolio valuation.

How accurate is the future crypto value estimate?

The arithmetic is based on your inputs, but the future growth rate is only an assumption. Crypto prices are volatile, and actual returns may be much higher or lower, including a total loss.

Can I use a negative growth rate?

Yes. Enter a negative annual percentage in any scenario to model a decline. For example, -10% applies a 10% annual decrease compounded over the selected period.

Does the calculator include staking rewards, taxes or trading fees?

No. It estimates the value of the holdings entered. It does not add staking or lending rewards, deduct trading fees, calculate tax, include new investments or account for withdrawals.

Can I use this calculator for cryptocurrencies other than Bitcoin?

Yes. Enter any coin or token name and ticker symbol. The calculator treats each row as a holding and uses the quantity, current price and optional average buy price you provide.

Is the bull scenario a price prediction?

No. The bull, base and bear values are hypothetical outcomes generated from growth rates selected by you. They are not predictions, recommendations or guaranteed returns.
