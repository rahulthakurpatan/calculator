HRA exemption formula in India

Under the old tax regime, the exempt house rent allowance is the lowest of these three amounts:

  1. Actual HRA received from the employer.
  2. Rent paid minus 10% of eligible salary.
  3. 50% of eligible salary for Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata or Chennai; 40% for other locations.

Eligible salary generally means basic salary, dearness allowance only where it forms part of retirement benefits, and commission where it is based on a fixed percentage of turnover.

HRA under old and new tax regimes

The HRA exemption is available under the old tax regime if the employee receives HRA and pays rent for the residential accommodation occupied. The new tax regime does not allow this HRA exemption. Actual tax savings depend on the full income and deduction profile.

Important conditions

  • No rent paid means no HRA exemption under this calculation.
  • Rent paid to a family member is not automatically disallowed, but it must be a genuine rental arrangement supported by evidence and actual payments.
  • Where annual rent exceeds ₹1 lakh, the landlord PAN is generally required for employer reporting / documentation.
  • If you move house, change city, receive a salary revision or have a rent-free period, calculate the relevant periods separately.
  • Home ownership and home-loan deductions are separate matters; they do not by themselves create HRA exemption.
This tool calculates HRA exemption only. It does not calculate your complete income tax liability or guarantee the employer will accept a particular rent claim.

Frequently asked questions

Can I claim HRA in the new tax regime?
No, the salary HRA exemption is not available under the new regime.

Is Bengaluru a 50% HRA metro?
This calculator uses the four cities in the official HRA guidance: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and Chennai. Do not apply an expanded metro list without a relevant official notification for the applicable tax year.

What if rent is lower than 10% of salary?
The rent-minus-10%-salary limb becomes zero or negative, so the HRA exemption is zero.

Can I calculate only three months?
Yes. Set the month count to the period represented by the entered monthly values. Run separate calculations if values change within the year.
