How this Dhan Yog calculator works

The calculator separates astronomical positions from traditional astrological interpretation, so you can inspect the basis of each result.

1. Birth chart and astronomical positions

The entered local birth time is converted to UTC using the UTC offset you select. Geographic coordinates are used to calculate local sidereal time and the ascendant. Planetary geocentric longitudes are calculated using the Astronomy Engine library and converted from tropical to sidereal longitude using an approximate Lahiri ayanamsa.

2. Houses and Parashari lordships

The chart uses whole-sign houses: the sign containing the ascendant becomes the 1st house, and each following sign becomes the next house. The classical seven planets rule signs using the traditional scheme; Rahu and Ketu are treated as shadow planets and are not assigned sign lordship in this calculator. Their positions use a mean lunar-node approximation.

3. Dhan Yog rules used

Traditional wealth analysis often examines the 2nd house (accumulated resources), 11th (gains), 5th (purva punya/intelligence) and 9th (fortune). This tool flags relationships among these house lords through conjunction, mutual sign exchange and classical Parashari aspects. It separately checks a simplified Lakshmi Yoga condition. Every result lists the rule that triggered it.

4. Vimshottari dasha

The Moon's sidereal longitude determines the birth nakshatra and the remaining balance of its planetary period. The sequence follows the standard 120-year Vimshottari cycle. The displayed current period is an approximate calendar calculation, not a prediction of a financial event.

The astronomy library is loaded from a public CDN when the calculator is opened. An internet connection is required for planetary calculations and city lookup. Your entered birth details are processed in the page and are not sent to InvestCalc servers by the calculator code.

Frequently asked questions

What astrology system does this calculator use?

It uses a sidereal zodiac with a Lahiri-style ayanamsa, whole-sign houses from the ascendant, Parashari planetary aspects and Vimshottari dasha conventions. Planetary longitudes are computed with a browser astronomy library; results are educational approximations, not a certified panchanga.

What is Dhan Yog in Vedic astrology?

Dhan Yog is a traditional astrological term for combinations involving wealth-related houses and their lords, commonly the 2nd, 5th, 9th and 11th houses. Interpretations vary among classical texts and astrologers.

Does a Dhan Yog guarantee wealth?

No. Astrology is not scientifically established as a way to predict wealth or financial outcomes. A chart reading should not replace financial planning or investment advice.

Why do I need an exact birth time and place?

Birth time and geographic coordinates affect the ascendant and house placements. An inaccurate time or timezone can change the chart and its interpretation.

Does the calculator save my birth details?

The calculator processes birth details in your browser and does not require an account. Do not enter information on a shared or untrusted device.

How accurate are the planetary positions?

Positions are calculated using the Astronomy Engine library and an approximate Lahiri ayanamsa. Small differences from professional ephemerides, true-node settings, time conversion and traditional calculation conventions may occur.
