1. Overview and who is responsible

Return on Investment operates the website returnoninvestmentcalculator.in (referred to as “we”, “us”, “our” or the “Website” in this policy). The website is maintained by Rahul Singh Thakur, based in Patan, Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh, India.

This policy applies to visitors who browse the Website, use its calculators, contact us, or choose to subscribe to browser notifications. It should be read together with our Terms and Conditions and Contact page.

We aim to collect and use only information that is reasonably needed to operate, understand, protect and improve the Website, respond to enquiries, and provide features that a visitor chooses to use. The exact information handled can depend on which page, browser settings and optional features you use.

2. Information we may handle

We do not require visitors to create an account to use the calculators currently available. Depending on how you interact with the Website, the following categories of information may be processed:

  • Contact information: If you email, call or message us, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, message contents and any information you choose to include.
  • Basic technical and usage information: Your browser and device type, operating system, approximate location inferred from an IP address, pages visited, referring page, date and time of access, interaction events and diagnostic information may be processed by the Website or its service providers.
  • Cookie and identifier information: Cookies, local storage, pixels, tags or similar technologies may store preferences, recognise a browser, support analytics, remember settings, measure advertising or help prevent abuse.
  • Notification subscription information: If you allow browser notifications, a push subscription or browser/device identifier and related delivery information may be processed by our notification provider.
  • Calculator information: Values you enter into a calculator, such as an investment amount, assumed return, duration or loan amount, are used to calculate the result displayed to you. See Section 3 for more detail.

Please do not send passwords, bank login credentials, card details, government identity numbers or other highly sensitive information in a general enquiry. We do not ask you to provide such information to use our calculators.

3. Calculator inputs and financial information

Our calculators are designed as browser-based tools. For the calculators that perform calculations directly in your browser, the values you enter are processed by the page's code to generate the result. We do not need those figures to identify you, and we do not intentionally ask you to submit bank account details or investment account credentials.

Some features you choose to use—such as contacting us, subscribing to notifications, or interacting with third-party services—may involve information being transmitted to the relevant provider. Browser extensions, network services, analytics tags or future features may also affect how technical information is processed. If a particular calculator or feature works differently, its page should explain any additional processing that is relevant to it.

Unless a calculator page clearly says otherwise, do not treat a value entered into a calculator as a confidential financial record stored by us. If you want to keep a result, use the available download, print or copy feature where provided and store it securely on your own device.

4. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

Cookies are small files placed on your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, web beacons, scripts and device or browser identifiers. These technologies may be used by us or by third parties whose services appear on the Website.

Why these technologies may be used

  • Essential operation: To support basic site functionality, security, load balancing or remember a necessary preference.
  • Analytics and measurement: To understand page visits, traffic sources, performance and how visitors use the Website.
  • Notifications: To remember or deliver a browser notification subscription if you opt in.
  • Advertising: If advertising services are enabled, cookies or similar identifiers may be used to deliver, limit, measure or personalise ads, subject to applicable consent requirements and your choices.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies or local storage may affect some website features. Browser controls do not necessarily remove information already held by a third-party provider or automatically opt you out of every form of analytics or advertising.

5. Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and measurement

The Website currently loads a Google Tag Manager script. Google Tag Manager is a tag-management service that can load or manage measurement and other tags configured by the site operator. The Website also includes a Google tag initialisation snippet. The information collected depends on which tags and settings are actually configured and published in the tag container.

Where Google Analytics or other analytics tags are enabled, they may process information such as pages viewed, approximate geographic region, device and browser details, referral information, interaction events and online identifiers. This information helps us understand site performance and improve the calculators and content. Google may process information as described in its own policies.

For more information, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can also review Google's Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, where supported.

6. Advertising, Google AdSense and third-party vendors

Return on Investment may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, to support the operation of the Website. Advertising availability and configuration may change. Where Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, the relevant provider and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers or similar technologies to serve, limit, measure and, where permitted, personalise advertisements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons and IP addresses to collect information as a result of ad serving on this Website. These technologies may help with ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, aggregated reporting and advertising measurement. Personalised advertising may use information about visits to this and other websites, subject to your consent and the provider's settings and policies.

Current-status clarification: This policy describes the advertising technologies that may apply when advertising is enabled. A privacy-policy disclosure does not itself mean that ads are currently being served, nor does it replace the need to configure advertising tags and consent controls correctly.

Google advertising and privacy choices

  • Manage Google's ad personalisation choices through My Ad Center, where available.

  • For information about Google's partner-site data practices, visit How Google uses information from partner sites.

Other advertising partners, if used, may have their own privacy policies and opt-out mechanisms. Where required, we will provide disclosures about the advertising technology providers configured for the Website.

Consent for visitors in regulated regions

For visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google requires publishers serving personalised ads to use a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). We must configure an appropriate consent experience and respect visitor choices before relevant advertising technologies operate. Please note that publishing this policy alone does not implement a CMP or record consent.

For visitors in India and other locations, we aim to follow applicable privacy, data-protection and advertising requirements. The applicable choices and disclosures may differ depending on your location and the services enabled.

7. Browser push notifications

The Website integrates a browser notification service provided by NameDotify / PushDotify. You are not required to subscribe. Your browser should ask for permission before notifications can be delivered; you can deny or revoke that permission through your browser or device settings.

If you subscribe, the notification provider may process a push subscription token or endpoint, browser/device information, delivery status and related technical data to send notifications. The provider's systems may process this information under its own terms and privacy practices. We may use the service to send website updates or announcements. We do not use browser notifications as a condition for using the calculators.

To stop notifications, change the notification permission for this Website in your browser or device settings. Removing permission may stop future notifications but may not immediately erase records already held by the notification provider. For provider-level deletion requests, contact the provider where its process permits.

8. When information may be shared

We do not sell visitors' personal contact information as a standalone mailing list. Information may be processed or shared in limited circumstances, including:

  • Service providers: Providers that help us operate the Website, manage tags, measure traffic, deliver browser notifications, host content, protect the site or respond to technical issues.
  • Advertising partners: Google and other advertising technology providers, if advertising services are enabled, may receive information through their tags, cookies or similar technologies as described above.
  • When you ask us to: If you choose to contact or interact with an external service, information may be shared with that service as part of your action.
  • Legal and safety reasons: Where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights, investigate abuse or maintain Website security.
  • Business changes: If the Website or its operation is reorganised or transferred, relevant information may be handled as part of that process with appropriate safeguards and notices where required.

Third-party providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies before using their services.

9. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate, maintain and improve the Website and its calculators.
  • Respond to questions, feedback, correction requests and support messages.
  • Understand website traffic, content performance and technical errors.
  • Send browser notifications when you have subscribed and permission remains active.
  • Support advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention if advertising services are enabled.
  • Protect the Website, detect misuse and comply with legal obligations.

We do not make a lending, credit eligibility, employment or insurance decision about you based on the figures you enter into a calculator.

10. Data retention and security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to an enquiry, maintaining security, meeting legal obligations or resolving a dispute. The retention period may vary by information type and by the policies of the relevant service provider.

Messages sent to our email or phone may remain in those communication systems until they are no longer needed or are deleted in accordance with the provider's controls. Analytics, advertising and push-notification providers may retain their own records under their respective retention settings and policies.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect the Website and information handled through it. However, no internet transmission, website, device or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please avoid sending confidential financial or identity information through general contact channels.

  • Cookies: Use your browser settings to block, limit or delete cookies and site data.
  • Analytics: Use available browser privacy controls and the Google Analytics opt-out add-on where applicable.
  • Advertising: Review Google's My Ad Center and the privacy controls offered by other advertising partners, where available.
