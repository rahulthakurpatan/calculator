1. Who operates this website

Return on Investment (also referred to as “we”, “us”, “our” or the “Website”) is an independent online calculator project founded and maintained by Rahul Singh Thakur, based in Patan, Madhya Pradesh, India.

This policy applies to the website at returnoninvestmentcalculator.in, including its calculator pages, learning tools and related website features. The website is not a bank, financial institution, government department or financial-advisory service.

2. Scope, visitors and accounts

You can browse the public pages and use the calculators without creating a website account. The website does not currently ask visitors to register, create a password, or provide bank or payment-card credentials to use its calculators.

Some information is processed automatically when a browser requests a web page. Other information is processed only when you choose to contact us, subscribe to browser notifications, use a browser feature, or interact with a third-party service. The exact information depends on your device, browser settings, the page used and the services enabled at that time.

3. Information that may be processed

Depending on how you use the Website, the following categories of information may be processed by us or by the service providers described in this policy:

  • Contact details and messages: Your name, email address, phone number and the contents of a message if you contact us. Any additional information you voluntarily include may also be received.
  • Technical and log information: IP address, browser and device type, operating system, language, referring page, requested URL, date and time, approximate location derived from an IP address, and diagnostic or security information. Hosting and security systems may create server logs to deliver pages and investigate errors or abuse.
  • Usage and interaction information: Pages visited, navigation, broad interaction events, referral sources, page performance and similar measurement information, if the relevant tags or analytics features are configured and active.
  • Cookie and browser-storage information: Cookie identifiers, local storage or session storage values, browser identifiers and similar technologies used for preferences, site functions, measurement, advertising or notification features.
  • Calculator information: Values you enter, such as investment amounts, assumed returns, durations, tax figures, costs or other inputs needed to calculate a result. See Section 4.
  • Push-notification information: If you subscribe, the browser push subscription endpoint or token, browser/device details, permission status and delivery-related information may be processed by the notification provider.
  • Notification interaction information: If a notification contains a campaign identifier and you click it, the website's notification click endpoint may receive that campaign identifier to record the click and process the requested destination.

Please do not send passwords, bank login credentials, full payment-card details, government identity numbers or other highly sensitive information through a general contact message. Such information is not needed to use the calculators.

4. Calculator inputs and information you enter

The calculators are designed primarily as browser-based tools. For a calculator that performs its calculation in the browser, the values you enter are processed by the page's code to display the result. A website account is not required for this calculation.

Some pages may offer convenience features such as remembering a preference, saving a scenario in the browser, or copying, printing or downloading a result. If a page uses browser storage, the relevant values may remain in that browser until they are cleared or overwritten. Browser storage is controlled through your browser's site-data settings. Do not use a shared device to store information you do not want another user of that device to see.

We do not intentionally ask visitors to submit their bank account credentials or investment account passwords. We do not represent that every calculator input is confidential or that every tool has identical storage behaviour. If a particular page explains an additional data transfer or feature, that page-specific explanation should be read together with this policy.

Unless a page clearly states otherwise, calculator results are estimates based on the inputs and assumptions shown on that page. Do not enter confidential personal or financial information into a calculator unless you are comfortable with the browser and page behaviour.

5. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

Cookies are small data files stored by a browser. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, pixels, web beacons, scripts and browser or device identifiers. These technologies may be set by the Website or by third parties whose services are used on a page.

How they may be used

  • Site operation and preferences: To support basic website functions, remember a setting, maintain a feature or help protect the Website.
  • Measurement: To understand visits, traffic sources, page performance and interactions when measurement tags are configured and active.
  • Advertising: Google and other advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, limit repeated ads, detect invalid activity, measure advertising performance and personalise ads where permitted.
  • Notifications: To maintain a browser push subscription and deliver a notification if you have opted in.

You can review, block or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies or storage may affect some features. Browser controls do not necessarily delete information already held by an independent third-party provider, and clearing cookies may not withdraw a consent choice stored elsewhere.

Where consent is required by applicable law, relevant non-essential storage or processing should only take place after the required consent has been obtained. A description in this policy is not itself a consent mechanism or a substitute for a working consent interface.

7. Google Tag Manager and website measurement

The Website loads Google Tag Manager. Tag Manager is a tool that can load and manage scripts or tags selected by the Website operator. The information processed depends on the tags, triggers and settings published in the Tag Manager container at the time of your visit.

Depending on the active configuration, tags may process page URLs, referral information, browser and device details, approximate location, timestamps and interaction or performance events. The presence of the Tag Manager loader does not by itself mean that every Google Analytics or marketing feature is active. Other tags may be added, changed or removed as the Website develops.

Google's information about its services is available in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner sites. If an analytics opt-out feature is available for a particular Google Analytics implementation, details are provided by Google at Google Analytics opt-out.

8. NameDotify / PushDotify browser notifications

The Website integrates the NameDotify / PushDotify browser push-notification service. Subscribing is optional and is not required to browse or use the calculators. Your browser or device controls whether notification permission is granted.

If you choose to subscribe, the notification provider may process a push endpoint or subscription token, browser and device information, permission status and technical delivery information to register your subscription and send notifications. The provider may process this information on its own systems and under its own terms and privacy practices.

Notifications may contain website updates, new content or announcements. If a notification includes a campaign identifier and you click it, the Website may receive that identifier to record the click and open the relevant destination. The notification provider may also process delivery and interaction information under its own practices.

You can stop future notifications by blocking or revoking notification permission for this Website in your browser or device settings. Revoking permission may not automatically erase subscription or delivery records already held by the provider. For provider-held information, use the provider's privacy or support process.
