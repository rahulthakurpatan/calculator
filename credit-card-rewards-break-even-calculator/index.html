How this calculator works

Annual reward value is 12 times the lower of the monthly reward cap and the weighted monthly reward. Net value subtracts the fee unless the specified annual eligible-spend threshold is reached, then adds the realistic value of used perks. The simple break-even spend estimate deliberately ignores caps and waivers.

Worked example

With 2,000 monthly spend, 30% in a 3% bonus category and the rest at 1%, estimated monthly rewards are 600 × 3% + 1,400 × 1% = 32, or 384 annually before fees and perks.

Assumptions and limitations

Use the current issuer terms. Merchant exclusions, points redemption value, category caps, fee taxes and fee-waiver eligibility vary by issuer. The simple break-even estimate is not valid if caps bind; compare the actual net value instead.

Calculations are educational estimates, not individualized financial advice. Verify current provider terms before making a decision.

Frequently asked questions

Does a fee waiver always apply?

No. Enter a waiver threshold only when your card's terms confirm it, and ensure your spending is eligible.

Are reward points worth cash?

Only at their actual redemption value. Enter equivalent cash-value percentages, not raw points earned.
