How to use this compound interest calculator

Four simple inputs are enough to estimate the maturity corpus of a one-time investment.

💰

1. Enter your principal

Add the amount you plan to invest once. This is the starting value used in the projection.

🎯

Use an assumed annual interest rate from your own scenario. The calculator does not predict or generate a rate.

Compound interest formula

The calculator uses the standard compound growth formula for a one-time investment.

Future value / Maturity corpusA = P × (1 + r ÷ n)n × t

P = principal · r = annual rate as a decimal · n = compounding periods per year · t = investment duration in years

What compounding frequency means

Compounding frequency describes how often accumulated interest is added to the balance.

📅

Yearly compounding

Interest is added once each year. It is a straightforward way to model annual compounding.

🌓

Half-yearly and quarterly

Interest is compounded two or four times a year, respectively, using the same nominal annual rate.

⚡

Monthly and daily

Interest is compounded more frequently. The result depends on the rate convention and actual product terms.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about corpus calculations and compounding.

What does a compound interest calculator calculate?

It estimates the future value of a one-time principal when interest is added to the balance at a selected frequency. The result is an estimated maturity corpus, not a guaranteed payout.

What inputs do I need to calculate my future corpus?

Enter your initial investment, expected annual interest rate, investment duration and compounding frequency. The calculator uses these assumptions to estimate the maturity value.

Does this calculator calculate the interest rate for me?

No. You provide the annual rate as an input. This tool calculates the estimated future corpus and shows the principal and estimated interest components.

Is monthly compounding always better than yearly compounding?

At the same nominal annual rate and under the same assumptions, more frequent compounding generally produces a slightly higher mathematical future value. Actual financial products may use different rate conventions and terms.

Can I use this calculator for an FD?

You can use it for an indicative projection if you enter the applicable rate and compounding convention. Bank FDs may have product-specific rules, payout options, taxes and rounding, so check the bank's maturity quotation for the exact amount.

Are mutual fund returns compounded at a fixed rate?

No. Mutual fund returns are market-linked and are not fixed or guaranteed. Any rate entered here is only a hypothetical assumption for illustration, not a forecast.

Does the result include tax, fees or inflation?

No. The displayed corpus is a gross mathematical estimate. It does not deduct tax, fees, inflation, exit charges or other product-specific costs.

Can I calculate a period shorter than one year?

Yes. Enter zero years and the number of additional months. The calculator supports a duration in years and months.
