SIP calculator formula and methodology

The estimate compounds regular contributions using a monthly rate derived from the effective annual return assumption.

Monthly rate = (1 + Annual return)1/12 − 1
FV = Σ [Monthly contribution × (1 + monthly rate)remaining months]

Monthly contribution (P): The amount invested at each monthly interval. Annual return (R): The effective annual rate entered as an assumption. Monthly rate (i): The equivalent monthly rate calculated as (1 + R)1/12 − 1. Number of instalments (n): Investment duration in years multiplied by 12.

For a fixed monthly contribution made at the end of each month, the equivalent annuity formula is FV = P × (((1 + i)n − 1) ÷ i). For a beginning-of-month contribution, each instalment receives one additional month of assumed growth. This tool calculates the monthly cash flows directly so it can also model an annual step-up contribution.

When the assumed return is 0%, the future value equals the sum of contributions. The goal mode solves for the monthly SIP using an iterative numerical method and uses the same contribution timing and step-up assumptions as the projection mode.

What the SIP calculator results mean

Understand the estimated figures before using them for financial planning.

🧾

Total amount invested

The sum of your monthly contributions. With a step-up SIP, this total increases as contributions rise each year.

🌱

Estimated wealth gain

The estimated future corpus minus the total amount contributed. It is not a guaranteed profit.

🎯

Inflation-adjusted value

An estimate of what the projected future corpus may be worth in today's purchasing power, using your inflation assumption.

Frequently asked questions about SIP

Common questions about SIP calculations, returns and investment assumptions.

What is a SIP calculator?

A SIP calculator estimates the future value of regular mutual fund contributions using the investment amount, duration and an assumed rate of return. It is a planning illustration, not a prediction of actual returns.

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund returns are market-linked and are not guaranteed. The calculator applies a constant assumed rate for mathematical illustration, while actual returns fluctuate with market and scheme performance.

How much SIP is required to reach ₹1 crore?

The monthly amount depends on the investment duration, assumed return, contribution timing and any annual step-up. Select Goal Amount above, enter ₹1 crore and adjust the assumptions to see an estimate. A higher assumed return is not guaranteed and involves investment risk.

How is the monthly return calculated?

This calculator converts the effective annual assumed return to a monthly rate using (1 + annual rate) to the power of 1/12, minus 1. This is different from simply dividing the annual rate by 12.

What is a step-up SIP?

A step-up SIP increases the monthly contribution at a regular interval, commonly once a year. Enter an annual increase percentage to model the higher contribution from each new investment year.

Does this calculator account for inflation?

It displays an approximate value in today's money by discounting the estimated future corpus using the inflation rate you enter. This is a simplified purchasing-power illustration, not a forecast of actual inflation.

Does the SIP calculator include taxes and mutual fund charges?

No. This estimate does not separately deduct taxes, expense ratios, exit loads, transaction costs or other scheme-specific charges. These can affect the actual amount received.

Should I use 12% as my expected return?

There is no universally correct return assumption. The appropriate assumption depends on the asset class, scheme, investment horizon and risk. Try more than one scenario and avoid treating any assumed rate as a promise.

Can I use this calculator for quarterly SIPs?

This version is designed for monthly contributions. For a quarterly investment, you can use the monthly mode only as an approximation by dividing or distributing the contribution across months; the timing will not exactly match a quarterly plan.
