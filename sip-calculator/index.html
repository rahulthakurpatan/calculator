How to use this SIP calculator

Three inputs are enough to estimate the future value of a regular monthly investment.

Enter the amount you plan to invest each month, such as ₹1,000, ₹5,000 or ₹10,000.

📈

🗓️

3. Set your time period

Enter how many years you expect to continue your monthly investments.

SIP calculator formula

This calculator uses the future value of a regular monthly investment (ordinary annuity).

FV = P × [((1 + i)n − 1) ÷ i]

Here, FV is the estimated future value, P is the monthly investment, i is the assumed monthly rate of return (annual rate divided by 12), and n is the total number of monthly investments.

When the assumed return is zero, the estimated future value is simply the monthly investment multiplied by the number of months.

Timing assumption: Contributions are assumed to be made at the end of each month. If you invest at the beginning of each month, the result may be slightly higher.

Understand your SIP estimate

Know what the calculator's three main figures mean before making an investment decision.

🧾

Total amount invested

This is the sum of all monthly contributions over the selected period. It does not include investment growth.

🌱

Estimated wealth gained

This is the difference between the estimated future value and the total amount invested.

🎯

Estimated future value

This is the projected value of your monthly contributions plus the assumed investment growth.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about monthly SIP calculations.

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a way to invest a fixed amount at regular intervals, commonly every month, in a mutual fund. The investment value can rise or fall with the market.

Are SIP calculator returns guaranteed?

No. The return rate is an assumption entered by you. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risk, and actual returns may differ from this estimate.

Does this calculator include taxes and mutual fund charges?

No. This basic estimate does not separately account for taxes, exit loads, expense ratios, transaction costs or changes in the investment amount.

Does the SIP calculator assume investment at the start or end of the month?

This calculator assumes each monthly contribution is made at the end of the month. Beginning-of-month contributions can produce a slightly different estimate.

Can I use this calculator for a 0% expected return?

Yes. At a 0% assumed return, the estimated future value equals the total amount you contribute over the selected period.

This calculator provides an illustration, not a prediction or guarantee. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks.
