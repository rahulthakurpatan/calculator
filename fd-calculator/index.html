Understand your FD calculation

Choose the deposit structure that matches the product offered by your bank.

Cumulative FD

Interest is added back to the deposit at the selected compounding intervals. The accumulated amount is generally paid at maturity.

Non-cumulative FD

Fixed deposit interest formula

The formula depends on whether interest is reinvested or paid out. This calculator uses the annual rate and tenure you enter to produce an estimate.

Cumulative FD – compound interestA = P × (1 + r ÷ n)n × tP = principal · r = annual rate as a decimal · n = compounding periods per year · t = tenure in years
Simple-interest estimateI = P × r × tUsed here as a simplified estimate for periodic non-cumulative interest payouts.

What can change your actual FD return?

  • Bank and deposit type: Interest rates and calculation rules vary by institution and product.
  • Tenure: The rate may depend on the exact deposit period, not just the number of years.
  • Senior-citizen rate: Some banks offer a different rate to eligible depositors. Enter the applicable quoted rate yourself.
  • Premature withdrawal: Closing an FD early may result in a lower applicable rate or penalty.
  • Tax and TDS: FD interest may be taxable. Tax treatment depends on your circumstances and current rules; this calculator shows pre-tax estimates.
  • Reinvestment: The result assumes the selected cumulative interest is reinvested according to the selected frequency.

Frequently asked questions

1. How is fixed deposit interest calculated?

For a cumulative deposit, interest is commonly estimated using compound interest. The amount depends on principal, annual rate, tenure and compounding frequency. Your bank's terms determine the actual calculation.

2. What is the difference between cumulative and non-cumulative FD?

A cumulative FD reinvests interest and generally pays the accumulated amount at maturity. A non-cumulative FD pays interest at selected intervals while the principal is generally repaid at maturity.

3. How often is FD interest compounded in India?

Many bank cumulative FDs use quarterly compounding, but the frequency can vary by bank and product. Always check the deposit terms.

4. Can I calculate monthly interest on an FD?

Yes. Select non-cumulative and choose monthly payout. The displayed amount is an estimate; a bank may calculate and discount monthly interest differently.

5. Does the calculator include senior-citizen benefits?

There is no automatic age-based rate adjustment. Enter the actual rate quoted to the eligible depositor, including any applicable additional rate.

6. Does this calculator deduct TDS or income tax?

No. Results are before tax and TDS. FD interest may be taxable under the rules applicable to the depositor.

7. Can I use this for a company or NRE/NRO deposit?

You can use it for a rough mathematical estimate, but deposit rules, eligibility, tax treatment, currency and payout terms may differ. Check the specific product documentation.

8. What happens if I withdraw my FD before maturity?

The bank may apply a premature withdrawal penalty or a lower interest rate based on the period the deposit actually remained invested. This calculator does not estimate those charges.

9. Is the maturity amount guaranteed?

No. This is an estimate based on the inputs and formula selected. The bank's deposit advice and applicable terms determine the actual maturity amount.

10. Is my financial information stored?

The calculation runs in your browser. This page does not ask for bank login details, account numbers or identity documents.
