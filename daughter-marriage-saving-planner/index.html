How the Daughter Marriage Saving Planner works

First, it estimates a current wedding budget from the selected city type, guest count, wedding scale, functions, jewellery preference and household setup. Next, it increases that estimate by the selected annual wedding-cost inflation assumption. Finally, it projects existing savings and calculates the monthly contribution required to work towards the future target.

Wedding budget estimate formula

Estimated future wedding cost = Current estimated cost × (1 + inflation rate)^years

City and package multipliers are broad planning proxies. Guest count influences venue and food costs, while the number of selected functions changes event-related costs. Jewellery and household setup are kept as separate optional components because family choices can vary widely.

Future savings formula

Future value = Existing savings × (1 + monthly return)^months + SIP future value

The SIP is simulated monthly. If you select an annual step-up, the monthly contribution increases at each 12-month interval. A numerical solver estimates the starting SIP needed to reach the target. The actual result depends on the selected assumptions and contribution timing used by the model.

Wedding cost planning for Indian families

There is no single correct price for an Indian wedding. A small hometown celebration with a limited guest list can cost much less than a multi-function hotel or destination wedding. Venue, food, guest count, jewellery, clothing, the number of functions and the city all make a meaningful difference. Published market guides also show wide variation across city tiers and wedding formats. Treat any online estimate as a starting point and confirm actual prices with local vendors before making commitments.

For this reason, the calculator presents its estimate as a planning range rather than a quotation. It uses a broad baseline and clearly adjustable assumptions. It does not claim to know the exact local price of a venue, caterer, gold item or photographer several years in advance.

Keep jewellery and household expenses separate

Gold and other jewellery can represent a significant and highly variable part of wedding spending. The planner allows a separate jewellery allowance so families can choose a modest, moderate, premium or custom budget. Future gold prices are not predicted by this tool. Household appliances and furniture are also optional, and should only be included if the family actually intends to budget for them.

Plan a celebration that fits your means

Guest count and the number of functions are two practical ways to shape the budget. Families can compare an intimate event with a larger celebration, combine functions where appropriate, and reserve money for genuine priorities. Do not treat a suggested budget, gift list or jewellery allowance as a social requirement. Dowry demands are unlawful; this planner does not estimate or encourage dowry.

How much monthly SIP is needed for a daughter's wedding?

The required SIP depends on the future wedding target, years remaining, current savings, one-time investment, annual return assumption and annual SIP increase. A longer saving period can reduce the starting monthly amount, while a higher future wedding-cost assumption can increase it. Use the scenario cards to test different assumed returns rather than relying on only one optimistic figure.

If the calculated SIP is not affordable, switch to the affordable monthly saving mode. The result will show the projected corpus and the remaining gap. You can then compare a smaller wedding budget, a longer timeline, an annual step-up or additional contributions. These are planning choices, not guarantees.

Important assumptions and limitations

  • Wedding-cost benchmarks are broad illustrative estimates and are not live vendor quotations. Actual costs vary by city, season, date, venue, menu, guest count and negotiation.
  • Inflation is a user-selected planning assumption. Wedding costs may rise faster or slower, and different categories such as gold, food and venue hire may move differently.
  • Investment returns are not guaranteed. Mutual funds and other market-linked investments can lose value. The planner does not recommend a fund or asset allocation.
  • Taxes, fund expense ratios, exit loads, transaction costs and changes in tax law are not included.
  • Jewellery and household setup are optional. No dowry, gifts demanded by another party or compulsory transfer is included or encouraged.
  • Use a qualified financial adviser for investment decisions and obtain written vendor quotations before booking.
This tool is for educational planning only. It is not investment advice, a wedding vendor quote, a gold-price forecast or a guarantee of future returns.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I save for my daughter's wedding in India?

It depends on the city, guest count, wedding functions, venue, food, jewellery and family preferences. Estimate a budget for your own plans rather than relying on one national average, then adjust it for future inflation.

What is a reasonable wedding budget for a middle-class family?

There is no fixed middle-class wedding budget. A small-town wedding with fewer guests can be much more affordable than a large multi-function event. Select your city type, guest count and wedding scale to get an indicative estimate.

How does the calculator estimate future wedding expenses?

It compounds the estimated current wedding budget by your selected annual wedding-cost inflation assumption for the number of years until the planned wedding.

Can I include gold jewellery and household items?

Yes. Jewellery can be set to basic, moderate, premium or custom. Household setup can be excluded or estimated at a basic, standard or complete level. The tool does not forecast future gold prices.

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. SIP is only a way to invest periodically. Market-linked returns fluctuate and can be negative. The return options are mathematical assumptions for illustration, not promised returns.

What if I cannot afford the required SIP?

Choose the affordable monthly saving option. The planner will estimate the corpus and shortfall. You can then compare a different guest count, fewer functions, a smaller budget, a longer timeline or an annual SIP step-up.

Does this planner include dowry?

No. Dowry demands are unlawful in India. The planner only covers voluntary event costs and optional household expenses selected by the family; it does not include or encourage dowry.
