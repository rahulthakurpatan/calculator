What is Return on Investment?

Return on Investment is an independent online financial tools platform at returnoninvestmentcalculator.in. It brings commonly needed money calculations into one place so that people can explore different scenarios before making financial decisions.

Money decisions often involve more than one number. A monthly investment can be affected by time, assumed returns, inflation and contribution increases. A bank deposit depends on its interest method and tenure. A loan involves the principal, interest rate, repayment period and total interest cost. Our calculators are designed to make these inputs and their estimated outcomes easier to see.

The platform is being developed as a broad financial calculation resource—not only a mutual fund or SIP website. Its coverage includes investment returns, mutual funds, savings products, banking, loans, tax-related calculations, family goals, business finance and other practical tools. Available tools and future plans are identified separately on this page.

Our mission

Our mission is to make financial calculations understandable and accessible. We want users to be able to test assumptions, compare possible outcomes and learn how different financial variables affect a result—without needing to work through complex formulas themselves.

We believe a useful calculator should do more than display a final figure. Wherever relevant, it should explain the inputs, show the calculation method, break results into meaningful parts and make important limitations visible.

How we approach our calculators

Clarity and honest assumptions matter as much as the final number.

Make inputs understandable

Each tool should explain what a user needs to enter and use sensible labels, units and ranges.

Show the calculation logic

Where useful, we explain the formula or method, and separate contributions, estimated growth, interest and costs.

Explain the result in context

We aim to show useful breakdowns, scenarios or year-wise projections instead of relying on a single headline figure.

What our estimates mean

A calculator result is only as useful as the inputs and assumptions behind it. For example, a mutual fund projection based on an assumed annual return is a mathematical illustration—not a forecast of market performance. A fixed deposit estimate depends on the rate, compounding frequency, tenure and product terms entered. A loan estimate may differ from a lender's final offer because fees, insurance, rate resets and lender-specific rules can affect the actual repayment.

We therefore aim to make assumptions visible and avoid presenting an estimate as a promise. When a calculation leaves out taxes, fees, inflation, market volatility or other factors, that limitation should be stated near the relevant tool.

Independence, transparency & financial responsibility

Return on Investment is a financial calculation and education platform. It is not a bank, mutual fund house, stockbroker, lender, insurance company or government authority. Unless a page clearly states otherwise, our calculators are general-purpose educational tools and do not provide personalised investment, tax, legal or accounting advice.

We do not promise investment returns, guarantee loan approval, endorse a particular mutual fund or ask users to invest based on a calculator result. Market-linked investments can lose value. Deposit rates, tax rules, product conditions and lending policies can change. Users should verify current terms with the relevant bank, financial institution, fund documents or qualified professional before acting.

We aim to keep tool descriptions, formulas and assumptions understandable. If you find a calculation issue, unclear explanation or broken link, please let us know through the website's contact channel so it can be reviewed.

Important: All projections are estimates based on user-entered values and simplified assumptions. Actual outcomes may differ. This website does not claim to be registered with SEBI or any other financial regulator as an investment adviser.

Frequently asked questions

Is Return on Investment free to use?

The calculators currently published on the website are available to use without a paid subscription. Check the individual tool page for any specific conditions.

Does Return on Investment recommend mutual funds or stocks?

No. Our calculators provide mathematical estimates and educational information. They do not select securities, recommend funds or provide personalised investment advice.

Are the returns shown by investment calculators guaranteed?

No. Any return entered into a calculator is an assumption used to illustrate a possible mathematical outcome. Actual market returns vary and can be lower or negative.

Can I use the calculators for financial decisions?

You can use them to explore scenarios and understand calculations. Before acting, verify current product terms, taxes, fees and risks with the relevant institution or a qualified professional.

Are all the financial categories already available?

No. Some categories contain published tools and others are part of the future roadmap. This page labels planned areas so visitors can distinguish them from live calculators.

How can I report a problem or suggest a calculator?

Use the website's Contact page to share a broken link, calculation concern or suggestion for a useful financial tool.
