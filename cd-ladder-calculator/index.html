How this calculator works

Each of five rungs receives one-fifth of the total. Rung i matures after i × entered interval months. Maturity value = principal per rung × (1 + annual nominal rate / compounding periods)^(periods × years). Sum the interest of all rungs, recognizing the payments arrive on different dates.

Worked example

A 25,000 ladder split equally into five rungs puts 5,000 in each. With a 12-month interval, the maturities fall at 12, 24, 36, 48 and 60 months. Each rung compounds at its own entered annual rate until its respective maturity.

Assumptions and limitations

This is an illustrative nominal-rate ladder, not bank-specific APY, early withdrawal or deposit insurance advice. The five maturities occur at different dates; their sum is not a single-date portfolio value. Reinvestment, taxes and penalties are excluded.

Calculations are educational estimates, not individualized financial advice. Verify current provider terms before making a decision.

Frequently asked questions

Why ladder CDs or FDs?

Staggered maturities can create more frequent access to cash while allowing different terms and rates.

Does this model automatically renew deposits?

No. It estimates each rung through its first maturity without automatic rollover.
