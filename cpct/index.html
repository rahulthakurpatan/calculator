How to prepare for the CPCT typing test

Start by confirming the keyboard input method on your computer. For Hindi practice, make sure the selected system layout produces readable Unicode Devanagari text in a plain text editor. Build accuracy with short passages before increasing your speed or choosing a longer timed session.

Use the practice pages to develop consistency, review repeated mistakes and become comfortable typing without looking at the keyboard. The available tools are independent learning aids, not an official CPCT examination platform.

Understand your practice results

Speed and accuracy displayed by this website are estimates calculated from the text entered during a practice attempt. They are useful for comparing your own sessions, but they are not an official CPCT score or a guarantee of exam performance. Always follow the latest instructions published by the examination authority.
