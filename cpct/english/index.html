English QWERTY practice

CPCT English Typing Test Online

Build English typing accuracy and speed with timed passages. Use short sessions to learn the rhythm, then practise for longer periods and compare your results.

How to use this English typing practice

  1. Select a test duration and passage.
  2. Start typing in the test area; the timer begins with your first keystroke.
  3. Keep your eyes on the passage and correct mistakes when needed.
  4. Review estimated speed, accuracy and errors after finishing.

Use a physical QWERTY keyboard for realistic practice. The results are personal practice estimates, not an official CPCT score.
