How to use the portfolio rebalancing calculator

Enter the market value of each asset class or holding in one currency and the percentage you want each to represent. Your target percentages must total 100%. For example, a two-fund portfolio might target 60% stocks and 40% bonds. The calculator shows allocation drift in percentage points and an action amount for each holding.

Formula and methodology

Current weight = current asset value ÷ current portfolio total × 100. Target value = target percentage × (current total + new cash). In full mode, trade = target value − current value; negative means sell and positive means buy. New cash is included in the target portfolio value, so total net purchases equal the contribution.

In buy-only mode, the calculator allocates the new contribution across underweight assets using a capped water-filling approach. No holdings are sold. When the contribution is too small to reach exact target weights, the result remains approximate; it reports the remaining drift. This mode is not a guarantee of reaching targets.

Worked example

Suppose stocks are worth $7,000 and bonds $3,000, with a 60%/40% target. With no new cash, the exact plan is to sell $1,000 of stocks and buy $1,000 of bonds. With $2,000 new cash and buy-only mode, the portfolio can reach the 60%/40% target by buying $200 stocks and $1,800 bonds. Both examples ignore transaction fees and taxes.

Country coverage and important assumptions

Works with USD, INR, GBP, EUR, CAD and AUD formatting for investors in the US, India, UK, Europe, Canada and Australia. All holdings must already be converted into the same selected currency. No live prices, exchange rates, tax rules, trading costs, bid/ask spreads, whole-share constraints or investment recommendations are included. Values are rounded for display; actual trades may vary with market movements. Rebalancing can create taxable gains; consult a qualified professional where appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rebalance without selling?

Yes. Select New money only and enter the contribution amount. The calculator directs purchases toward underweight holdings, without selling any positions.

Do target percentages need to add up to 100%?

Yes. A different total produces a validation message rather than silently normalizing your targets.

Is this the same as a portfolio growth projection?

No. This tool calculates current trades to reach a chosen allocation. Our Investment Portfolio Planner models future portfolio outcomes, and our Net Worth Calculator estimates assets less liabilities.

Is rebalancing tax-free?

Not necessarily. Selling appreciated investments may create taxable gains, depending on your jurisdiction and account. This tool does not calculate taxes.
