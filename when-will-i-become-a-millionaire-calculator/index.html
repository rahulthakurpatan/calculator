    How the millionaire calculator works

    This calculator estimates when your projected net worth may reach a selected wealth target. It combines your current assets and liabilities with a monthly investment plan. You can model a local-currency millionaire goal, a US-dollar millionaire goal or a custom amount.

    1. Calculate your current net worth

    Current net worth = Cash + Investments + Retirement assets + Property + Other assets − Debts

    Enter the full market value of property and separately enter the outstanding mortgage. Do not enter property equity and the full mortgage balance together, because that would count the loan twice. Keep investments separate from cash and other assets.

    2. Project your invested portfolio

    The existing investment portfolio and retirement investments form the starting invested balance. Monthly contributions are added at the end of each month, the monthly contribution can increase annually, and an optional extra lump sum is added at each year-end.

    Monthly rate = (1 + annual net return)1/12 − 1

    Annual net return is the expected annual effective return minus the annual fee assumption. This model uses monthly compounding and end-of-month contributions. Actual products may compound or charge fees differently.

    3. Include other assets and liabilities

    Cash, property and other assets are shown separately from the invested portfolio. The optional property / other asset growth assumption is applied to property and other assets. Outstanding debts can be held constant or reduced using the simplified annual debt-reduction assumption. This is a planning simplification, not an amortisation schedule.

    4. Understand inflation-adjusted wealth

    A future target can look large in nominal currency but buy less than the same amount today. The calculator discounts future wealth by the inflation assumption to show estimated present purchasing power. You can also select a custom target that is already adjusted for your desired future lifestyle.

    5. Understand the assumptions

    • Returns are not guaranteed; actual returns vary and can be negative.
    • Income growth is used only for savings affordability guidance. It is not assumed that every salary increase is invested.
    • Tax on investment gains is not calculated separately. The annual fee field is a simplified drag, not a tax estimate.
    • Property and business assets may be illiquid and their future value is uncertain.
    • Currency conversion is a user-editable planning rate, not a live exchange rate.

    Frequently asked questions

    When will I become a millionaire?

    It depends on your current net worth, investment balance, monthly contributions, contribution increases, returns, fees, inflation and the target currency. Enter your details above to estimate a possible year and age. The result is a scenario, not a promise.

    Does millionaire mean one million in my local currency or one million US dollars?

    The default is one million in your selected display currency. You can switch to USD millionaire mode, which converts US$1 million using an editable exchange-rate assumption, or enter a custom target.

    Does this calculator include my house and other assets?

    Yes. You can include property, business, vehicle, gold and other assets, along with outstanding loans. Property value and mortgage debt are entered separately. The result also distinguishes invested assets from other net assets because property is not the same as liquid investment wealth.

    Should I include my retirement account?

    Include retirement or pension investments if you want them counted toward your total net worth. Remember that access restrictions, withdrawal taxes and penalties may apply in your country.

    How does inflation affect my millionaire goal?

    Inflation reduces purchasing power over time. The calculator shows the estimated present purchasing power of your projected wealth and can convert a US-dollar target to your display currency using your own exchange-rate assumption.

    Are the investment returns guaranteed?

    No. The return input is an assumption. Actual returns depend on the investment, market conditions, fees, taxes, timing and risk. Conservative and optimistic scenarios are illustrations only.

    Does the calculator automatically invest my salary increases?

    No. Income growth is used to estimate future income and a possible contribution level based on your expenses and debt payments. Only the monthly investment contribution and annual contribution increase are used in the wealth projection.

    What if my current net worth is negative?

    A negative net worth is valid when liabilities exceed assets. The calculator still projects your investment portfolio and other net assets, but a large debt balance can delay the date at which total net worth reaches your target.
