How loan amortization works

Principal

The amount that reduces what you owe. With level payments, the principal portion generally grows as the balance falls.

Interest

Interest is calculated on the outstanding balance using the periodic rate and convention selected above.

Extra principal

Additional payments can reduce future interest and shorten repayment when credited directly to principal.

Loan amortization formula

Payment = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

P is principal, r is periodic interest rate and n is the number of payments. At zero interest, payment equals principal divided by n. Each period's interest equals opening balance × periodic rate; principal equals payment minus interest.

Country and lender conventions

Loan contracts vary. Canadian fixed-rate mortgages commonly use semi-annual compounding conventions. Other products may use nominal, effective or daily interest. Select the method matching your agreement. This tool does not reproduce every lender's day-count, fees, prepayment limits or rounding rules.
